Features

Everything an Assamese meal needs

From the mashed pitikas we’re named after to curries, thalis and sweets.

🥔

Pitika, the core product

Potato, brinjal or tomato mashed by hand with raw mustard oil, onion and green chilli. Simple, sharp and the reason we exist.

🐟

Masor tenga

Light, sour fish curry with tomato and lemon. The classic way to finish a meal.

🍗

Chicken and duck curries

Kukura mangxo and haah mangxo, slow-cooked in home-style gravies.

🍛

Thali

Rice, dal, bhaji, pitika and a curry on one plate. Veg, fish and chicken versions.

🍮

Sweets and tea

Payokh, til pitha, narikol laru and strong Assam tea to wrap up.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Where is Pitika?

Garchuk – Pamohi Rd, opposite UCO Bank or Brahmaputra Academy, near Leena’s Dental Clinic, Garchuk Chariali, Guwahati, Assam 781035. Plus code: 4P85+2G.

How much does a meal cost?

Most diners spend ₹200–400 per person, as reported by 61 people on Google.

Do you do dine-in and takeaway?

Yes, both.

Is there parking?

Yes. There is a free parking lot with plenty of space.

Is Pitika good for kids and groups?

Yes. The atmosphere is casual, it works well for groups and it is good for kids.

Can I pay with my phone?

Yes, NFC mobile payments are supported.

What are your opening hours?

We’ll publish hours here soon. Check Google Maps for the latest.

What does “pitika” mean?

It is the Assamese way of mashing: vegetables, fish or potato crushed by hand with mustard oil, onion and chilli.

Ready to taste Assam?

Come hungry. We’re in Garchuk, close to Garchuk Chariali.
